# About Cat Royale

## Why this game exists

Cat Royale was made for one player: a 12-year-old who loves two games, **The Battle Cats** and **Clash Royale**. Instead of choosing between them, Cat Royale mashes them together. It plays like Clash Royale (real-time lane battles, a deck of cards, a resource that fills up, towers to knock down) but looks and feels like Battle Cats (goofy white blob cats holding weapons, silly enemies like dogs and penguins, cats that evolve into stronger forms, and a campaign that marches from the backyard to the Moon).

It runs in a web browser, so it works on an iPhone with no App Store, no account, and no real-money purchases. Add it to the Home Screen and it opens like a real app.

## The goal for the player

**Conquer all eight worlds and defeat the Alien Emperor.**

1. Build a deck of 8 cats and spells.
2. Battle NPC opponents in 3-minute matches: send cats up the lanes, knock down the enemy Doghouses, and smash the Big Doghouse to win.
3. Earn Cat Food and Catnip from every win, then spend them to level cats up and evolve them (Normal → Evolved → True Form).
4. Beat each world's boss to unlock the next world and a guaranteed new cat.
5. Collect every cat, including the rare ones from the Toy Capsule Machine and the exclusive Facetime Cats that only the Game Master can give.

The worlds, in order: Backyard, Alley, City Park, Rooftops, Frosty Peaks, Haunted Mansion, Robot Factory, The Moon. Each has five stages. Enemies get tougher, smarter, and pick up traits (Red, Floating, Metal, Alien) that reward choosing the right cats.

## The goals behind the design

- **Quick, satisfying matches.** Three minutes, one screen, no waiting. Double Kibble in the last minute and Overtime keep every match tense to the end.
- **Real decisions, not just tapping.** Kibble is scarce, so when and where you play a card matters. Cats have roles (tank, damage, splash, anti-air, tower rusher, support) and counters, the same skills Clash Royale teaches.
- **Always something to work toward.** New cats to unlock, levels to buy, forms to evolve, paws to earn on every stage, daily rewards and quests, and a final boss at the end.
- **Fair by design.** There is nothing to buy with real money. Every currency is earned by playing. The Shop's Venmo link and promo codes are a fun way for the parent to hand out bonuses, not a paywall.
- **A parent's lever.** Gift codes let the Game Master reward good stuff in real life with something the player genuinely wants in the game.
- **Made to grow.** The battle engine treats both sides identically, so player-versus-player could be added later. New cats, enemies, and worlds are one data entry each.
